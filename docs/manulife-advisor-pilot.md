# BullBrief for Manulife advisors

## The pitch

BullBrief is the daily advisor intelligence and compliance workflow that sits between market data and client action.

Traditional platforms help advisors research products, manage portfolios, build plans or create proposals. BullBrief focuses on the work that happens every morning and whenever markets move:

1. Detect what changed.
2. Identify which holdings or product reviews may need attention.
3. Prepare accurate client conversation points.
4. Complete KYC, KYP, suitability and conflict checks.
5. Preserve the rationale and follow-up record.

## Demonstrable differentiation

### Signal to record

One click converts current market moves, current news and available portfolio symbols into:

- verified market facts;
- affected-holding review prompts;
- client conversation points;
- KYC and KYP follow-up;
- suitability, concentration and liquidity checks;
- conflict and documentation prompts.

Missing client or product information is identified instead of assumed.

### Canadian compliance workflow

BullBrief connects daily market monitoring to the advisor's Canadian regulatory workflow. It keeps KYC and KYP distinct, flags suitability triggers, and prompts documentation without claiming to make the firm's compliance determination.

### Current-data AI with evidence boundaries

Bull AI is restricted to supplied market, news and portfolio context for current facts. It separates verified facts from interpretation and refuses to invent KYC, tax, cost basis, position size, product shelf or suitability information.

### Advisor-first daily workspace

The product opens to portfolio exceptions, KYP reviews, market developments and client conversations. It does not force an advisor to begin with a research terminal or a financial plan.

## Competitive position

| Platform category | Core strength | BullBrief position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morningstar Direct Advisory Suite | Investment data, research, planning, proposals and portfolio analysis | Use Morningstar data inside a faster daily action and KYP workflow |
| CapIntel | Comparisons, proposals, presentations and compliance checkpoints | Focus earlier in the workflow on daily signals, client preparation and review records |
| Croesus and d1g1t | Enterprise portfolio management, trading, reporting and operations | Complement the system of record with a lightweight advisor intelligence layer |
| Conquest | Goals-based planning and strategy modeling | Complement long-range planning with daily market and product workflows |

BullBrief should be positioned as an intelligence and workflow layer that integrates with established systems, not as a replacement for custody, trading, CRM or enterprise portfolio accounting.

## Recommended pricing

### Enterprise pilot

CAD $25,000 for 90 days and up to 50 advisors.

Includes:

- configured Manulife-branded workspace;
- advisor desktop, KYP workflow and Signal to Record;
- one approved portfolio or watchlist data connection;
- onboarding and weekly pilot review;
- usage and workflow reporting;
- documented success criteria;
- pilot support.

Third-party market and Morningstar data licensing is priced separately or supplied through the institution's existing agreement.

### Enterprise production

CAD $60 to $95 per advisor per month, billed annually, with a CAD $60,000 annual platform minimum.

Pricing varies by:

- advisor volume;
- SSO and identity requirements;
- CRM, portfolio and document integrations;
- data licensing;
- retention and audit requirements;
- service level and support;
- custom compliance controls.

### Independent advisor edition

CAD $149 per advisor per month, or CAD $1,490 annually.

Includes the advisor desktop, portfolio monitoring, grounded Bull AI, KYP records, PDF output, news context and daily compliance checks. Premium data feeds remain separate.

## Pilot success measures

- time from market event to advisor-ready client brief;
- time required to complete and document a KYP review;
- percentage of daily checks completed;
- reduction in manual research and copy-paste steps;
- advisor adoption and weekly active use;
- percentage of generated briefs accepted without factual correction;
- compliance reviewer satisfaction with evidence and terminology;
- number of client conversations prepared through BullBrief.

## Suggested close

BullBrief is not another destination advisors have to manage. It is the layer that tells them what changed, which clients may need attention and what must be documented next. The proposed 90-day pilot gives Manulife a controlled way to test advisor adoption, compliance fit and measurable time savings before an enterprise rollout.
